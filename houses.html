The only finished house in the district you can finance. It sits CHF 54,000 under the ceiling and lands at 31% strain — inside the limit, with nothing to spare.

Four floors for 169 m², so the space is vertical and the plot is small for the money. The 5.5 rooms need a lightweight wall rebuilt; today it is 4.5. Garage and two outdoor spaces are included, which is unusual at this price.

What decides it

“Continuously maintained and renovated” is the seller’s phrase, not a fact. At 40 years old the heating, roof, windows and bathrooms are all at end of life unless proven otherwise — potentially CHF 80,000–150,000 paid in cash you don’t have spare.

This is land, not a house — the listing itself says the building suits demolition. Buying it and living in it while you save is cheap: 21% strain, and below CHF 1,270,000 the bank doesn’t even require amortization.

The rebuild is where it binds. A finished value near CHF 1,900,000 needs roughly CHF 700,000 of own funds to stay inside the limit — about CHF 300,000 more than you have. Living here instead of renting is what generates it, over four or five years rather than two.

The interim is 71 m² across two floors with one bathroom and no real insulation. Spend nothing beyond making it habitable.

What decides it

The bank values a declared teardown at land value less demolition, not at what you pay. A valuation of CHF 950,000–1,100,000 against a bid of CHF 1,250,000 puts the gap on your cash — the same cash the rebuild depends on. Ask UBS before you bid.

The best plot in the set by a wide margin, and the only one that earns money. The separate 3.5-room apartment has its own external staircase and is let-ready.

It doesn’t finance at 400,000. With CHF 20,000 a year of rent credited you’d need about CHF 461,000 — a gap of 61,000. Without the rent credited, 518,000. Whether UBS counts that income, and at what haircut, is a single question to Beller.

The 1979 interior is original and the renovation is not covered by the mortgage.

What decides it

The auction has no ceiling. Every CHF 100,000 of bidding costs roughly CHF 70,000 more in own funds. Fix your walk-away number before you register.
