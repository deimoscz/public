Score is a weighted mean of the ticked factors: depreciation ×3, maintenance ×1.5, reliability ×1.5, luxury ×1, excitement ×1, features ×1. Value is that score divided by the candidate price, rescaled across all 16 models so the best quality-per-franc reads 5.00 and the worst 1.00. The rank number always reflects Score, whatever the table is sorted by.

Depreciation is derived, not judged — band midpoint in CHF/yr mapped linearly, 3'250 → 5.00 and 7'930 → 1.00, shown to two decimals. The BYD Seal figure is exact: CHF 42'790 new against a stated CHF 19'000 residual after three years. The other five ratings are judgement, relative to this field only, shown to one decimal. They sort well but won't decide between two close cars.

Open marks a model with finalists but no fixed candidate. Tax is the Schwyz formula, ±10% on actual kerb weight; energy assumes 12'500 km/yr.
