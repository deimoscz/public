Score is a weighted mean of the ticked factors: depreciation ×3, maintenance ×1.5, reliability ×1.5, luxury ×1, excitement ×1, features ×1. Value is that score divided by candidate price, rescaled across all 16 so best quality-per-franc reads 5.00. The rank number always reflects Score, whatever the sort.

3-yr cost = depreciation + tax + energy + a tyre and service allowance, over three years. Stability shows best and worst Score rank across seven weightings — a narrow range means the car doesn't depend on one configuration to look good.

Depreciation is derived: band midpoint mapped 3'250 → 5.00, 7'500 → 1.00. The BYD figure is exact — CHF 39'800 against a stated CHF 19'000 residual. The other five ratings are judgement, relative to this field only. Open marks a model with finalists but no fixed candidate.
