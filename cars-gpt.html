How to read this tool

  1. Score is the weighted mean of the factors currently ticked above; weights are renormalised over only the included factors. Value is Score ÷ price and is then min–max rescaled to 1.00–5.00 across the full 17-record set, never just the filtered rows.
  2. The rank number always reflects Score, even when you sort the table by price, mileage, date or another column.
  3. 3-year cost = (estimated depreciation midpoint + Schwyz road tax + energy + wear/service allowance) × 3. Energy assumes 15'000 km/year and CHF 0.35/kWh, using a realistic planning-consumption assumption including charging losses. Insurance and financing are deliberately excluded.
  4. Stability is the best–worst Score rank reached across seven fixed weighting scenarios: the default weighting plus six scenarios where one factor is ×3 and every other factor is ×1. It is computed on all 17 model records.
  5. Depreciation rating is derived: each candidate's forecast depreciation-band midpoint is mapped linearly across this field, cheapest/year = 5.00 and dearest/year = 1.00. The other five ratings are judgement calls relative to this field only and are shown to one decimal.
  6. These ratings are useful for sorting, but are not precise enough to decide between two close records. Listing facts, warranty terms, condition, battery health, exact equipment and a pre-purchase inspection are more reliable than a 0.1-point score difference.
Data provenance. Listing price, date/vintage, mileage, seller and explicitly advertised equipment/warranty come from the linked Swiss AutoScout24 inventory snapshot; where only a model/dealer search page could be verified, the popup says so explicitly. Model specifications use manufacturer or high-quality technical sources. Schwyz EV road tax is modelled from the canton’s weight-based EV formula; because the actual Swiss registration document controls taxation, treat the tax number as a planning estimate. Missing listing-specific data is shown as “—”, never silently filled.
